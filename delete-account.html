Delete Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's how to delete your YoutuLearn account and what happens when you do.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed within 48 hours:

  • Your profile information (name, email)
  • All saved vocabulary and SRS progress
  • Watch history and video preferences
  • Community suggestions and votes
  • Subscription data (your subscription itself must be cancelled separately through your app store)

⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

How to delete your account

Method 1 — In the app

Open YoutuLearn → Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account". You'll be asked to type "DELETE" to confirm. Your account will be removed within 48 hours.

Method 2 — By email

Send an email to maiphamtn@hotmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will process your request within 48 hours.

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it first through your app store to avoid future charges.
