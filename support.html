Help & Support

Find answers to common questions below. Can't find what you need? Contact us directly.

How do I save a word?

+
While watching a video, tap any word in the subtitle transcript. A dictionary popup will appear with the definition and translation. Tap the Save button to add it to your vocabulary notebook.

How does spaced repetition work?

+
Saved words are scheduled for review using a 7-level SRS system. Words you know well appear less often, while difficult words show up more frequently. Practice with flashcards or dictation from the Vocabulary tab.

Can I use the app offline?

+
You need an internet connection to watch videos and look up words. However, your saved vocabulary and flashcards are available offline once loaded.

How do I change the interface language?

+
Go to Profile → Language and select your preferred language. YoutuLearn supports 13 interface languages including English, Vietnamese, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, French, German, and more.

How do I suggest a video?

+
Go to the Community tab and tap Submit. Paste a YouTube URL, select a category, and optionally add a reason why it's good for learning. Other users can vote on your suggestion.

Is YoutuLearn free?

+
Yes! YoutuLearn is free to use with a short ad before each video. Premium subscribers get ad-free access to all content and features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

+
Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play Store → Subscriptions. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of your billing period.
