Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

YoutuLearn ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

01

Information we collect

We collect the following information when you use YoutuLearn:

  • Email address and name — provided when you sign in with Google or Apple
  • Display name — chosen or edited by you in your profile
  • Learning progress — saved vocabulary, watch history, SRS review data
  • Community contributions — video suggestions and votes
  • Device information — platform (iOS/Android), app version
  • Push notification token — to send learning reminders (optional)
02

How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the YoutuLearn learning experience
  • To save your vocabulary and sync progress across devices
  • To display community video suggestions and vote counts
  • To send optional push notifications and learning reminders
  • To manage your subscription status via RevenueCat
  • To respond to support requests
03

Third-party services

YoutuLearn uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management
  • Google AdMob — advertising (shown to free users only)
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — authentication
  • YouTube — video playback via embedded iframe (we do not download videos)
  • Cloudflare — subtitle file delivery via CDN
  • MyMemory — anonymous translation API for word definitions

Each provider has their own privacy policy governing their data practices.

04

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to operate the services listed above, or as required by law.

Community video suggestions (title, category, reason) are visible to other users. Your email address is never publicly displayed.

05

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your personal data, vocabulary, watch history, and associated records are permanently removed within 48 hours.

You may also request deletion by emailing maiphamtn@hotmail.com.

06

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at maiphamtn@hotmail.com.

07

Children's privacy

YoutuLearn is intended for users aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

08

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at maiphamtn@hotmail.com.
